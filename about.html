Our story

Small school energy.
Big possibilities.

Founded in 2011, Golden Gate Pentagon College began as a bold idea: that learning should feel alive, personal, and full of wonder. Today we serve families across early years, primary, and secondary levels, blending strong academics with creative studios, outdoor exploration, and community connection.

Every child is known by name. Every question is worth pursuing. And every classroom is a place where curiosity is the curriculum.
